<p align="center">

</p>

---

### What you will find here
- `drawings/`: 2D technical drawings, tolerances, and templates.
- `3d_exports/`: Mechanical models (STEP) exported from the PCB and additional mechanical mounts.

### Guidelines
> The mechanical envelope and center of mass must respect the PC/104 standard or the standard established by the CubeSat.
