<h1 align="center">3D Models</h1>

<p align="center">

</p>

<br>

### What you will find here
- `.step` exports of the assembled Printed Circuit Board (PCBA).
- 3D models of any mounts, shields, and testing jigs.

### Guidelines
> When exporting from the PCB, simplify the models of smaller electronic components to avoid overloading the main mechanical assemblies.
